## Stories used on the poster (all verified 25 Nov 2026)

### LEAD — Strait of Hormuz: tankers hit mines
Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said two oil tankers struck naval
mines and exploded and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, while trying to
transit an unauthorised route. Reported by Just Security, Open Chronicle,
One Turn. The day's biggest story.

### Pakistan's first astronaut reaches orbit
China launched Shenzhou-24 on a Long March-2F rocket from Jiuquan at 08:18
Beijing time, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali,
40 — the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to visit
China's Tiangong space station. Docked at 11:56 Beijing time, about 3.5 hours
after liftoff. Reported by Reuters, AP, Xinhua.

### Huawei convicted in the US
A New York federal jury found Huawei guilty of bank fraud and conspiracy to
steal trade secrets, but not guilty of the overarching racketeering charge.
Reported by Just Security, Principia, One Turn.

### Asian tech stocks tumble
Japan's Nikkei fell 2.8%, South Korean shares fell more than 6% at one point,
SoftBank fell about 15%, on renewed doubts over the AI boom. Reported by
One Turn, Principia.

### Ukraine war: leaked death toll
Leaked Russian government statistics analysed by BBC News show at least
490,334 Russian soldiers have died since the war began in 2022. Separately,
a Russian strike on Odesa region killed two people and injured three. Also:
Ukraine said its drones sank a Russian warship in a Libyan port; two Libyan
officers died. Reported by Just Security, Principia, One Turn.

### Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia
Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthis said they attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil
refinery; a Houthi strike on a skyscraper in Riyadh injured 11 people.
Reported by Just Security, One Turn.
